# Optic Canvas Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Optic Canvas is an image utility for Mac, iPhone and iPad, developed by Sushanth Reddy.

## Images and documents

Image conversion, editing, background processing, text recognition and AI upscaling run on your device. Optic Canvas does not upload your images or documents to the developer or an AI service. The app accesses files you choose and writes results to the destination you select. A selected cloud drive or file provider may transfer files according to that provider's policies.

## Preferences and purchases

Preferences and favorites are stored locally. Apple handles payment, billing and purchase restoration. Optic Canvas uses Apple's StoreKit framework to verify access to Pro features. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app. Apple's handling of Apple Account and purchase information is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Tracking and analytics

Optic Canvas does not include third-party advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. It does not require an Optic Canvas account. Apple may provide developers with diagnostic or sales information under your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

## Support and external links

If you contact support or post a GitHub issue, information you choose to provide is used to respond to your request. GitHub issues are public: do not include private photos, payment details or other sensitive information. GitHub and any external website you open have their own privacy policies. [Contact support](https://github.com/sushanth21042004/optic-canvas-support/issues).

## Retention and control

The developer does not operate a server storing your imported images or documents. You control exported files and can delete them through Files or Finder. Local settings remain on your device until cleared or removed with the app as supported by the operating system. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

## Changes

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date. Questions about privacy can be raised using the support link above without including personal or sensitive information.
